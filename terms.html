ClipGTA6 – Terms of Service

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. About the application

ClipGTA6 is a personal, non-commercial tool used by a single creator to prepare short vertical video clips
and send them as drafts to the creator's own TikTok account, using the official TikTok Content Posting API.
The application is not offered to the public and has no other users.

2. Use of TikTok

The application only uploads videos to the TikTok inbox (drafts) of the account that explicitly authorized it.
Nothing is published automatically: the account owner reviews each draft and decides whether to publish it
from the TikTok app. Use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

3. Responsibility for content

The account owner is solely responsible for the content they choose to publish, including obtaining any
permissions required from the original rights holders.

4. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions).
